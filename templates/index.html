How DigitVision AI Works

Understanding the deep learning pipeline behind handwritten digit recognition.

1. MNIST Dataset

70,000 benchmark images of handwritten digits (0–9) collected by MNIST. Standardized to 28x28 grayscale format.

2. Image Preprocessing

Images are grayscale converted, auto-inverted if light-backgrounded, anti-alias resized to 28x28, and normalized (x/255).

3. Neural Architecture

Sequential network with Flatten layer, 256-neuron & 128-neuron ReLU hidden layer, and 10-neuron Softmax output layer.

4. Softmax Inference

Computes probability distribution across all 10 digit classes. Selecting class with highest probability.
